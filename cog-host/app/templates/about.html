About Cog Host

Cog Host combines results from several open-source tools and free APIs into one local dashboard for self-audit and contact verification: checking your own footprint, verifying a recruiter or work contact, or checking an unknown caller or scam sender.

Notice. Looking up other people means handling personal data. In the UK, the GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 apply once this goes beyond purely personal or household use, and the Computer Misuse Act 1990 applies to anything that bypasses access controls (logging in as someone else, scraping behind a login, automating a platform against its terms). Cog Host is built to stay on the right side of that line: no scraping, no bulk lookups, no logging in as you to view someone else's account, and breach checks return names only, never contents. This is not legal advice.

What this deliberately does not do

  • No LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram scraping, and no automation of a logged-in session
  • No unofficial WhatsApp client libraries
  • No physical address lookup, data-broker or people-search sites
  • No leaked-credential contents, only breach names
  • No bulk upload, CSV import, or scheduled monitoring of a person over time
  • Never exposed to the public internet
